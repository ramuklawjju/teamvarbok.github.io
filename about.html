Learn About Us

A second home where little hearts learn to bloom

Varbok began in 2014 in a sunlit Bengaluru bungalow, founded by three Indian educators who believed childhood should be felt before it is measured. After years inside rigid classrooms, they dreamed of a place where children could climb, paint, sing and ask a hundred questions without ever being told to sit still and stay quiet.

  • Learning through joyful, hands-on play
  • Safe, CCTV-monitored campuses
  • Trained, loving caregivers and teachers

Our philosophy is simple: children learn best through play. Mud kitchens, music circles, storytelling corners and open gardens replace worksheets and rote drills. Every activity is designed to build curiosity, language, motor skills and emotional confidence, gently preparing little ones for school and for life with joy rather than pressure.

Above all, Varbok is a community built on trust and safety. With CCTV-monitored campuses, trained caregivers, nutritious home-style meals and an open-door relationship with every parent, we have grown into a second home for hundreds of families who simply want their child to feel safe, seen and celebrated.

Our Facilities

Everything your child needs to thrive

Safe Outdoor Play

Fenced, soft-surface play gardens with age-appropriate climbers and swings, where children build strength, balance and confidence under the watchful eye of trained caregivers.

Music & Movement

Daily rhythm circles with drums, shakers and song help little ones develop language, coordination and self-expression, turning every morning into a joyful celebration of sound.

Arts & Crafts Studio

A colourful, mess-friendly studio where children paint, mould and create freely, strengthening fine-motor skills, imagination and the quiet pride of making something with their own hands.

Safe Transport

GPS-tracked, attendant-supervised vans with trained drivers ferry children door to door, giving busy parents complete peace of mind on every single journey to and from Varbok.

Healthy Meals

Freshly cooked, home-style vegetarian meals and snacks, planned by a nutritionist, nourish growing bodies while gently teaching children healthy eating habits and happy mealtime manners.

Learning Trips

Guided outings to farms, parks and bakeries bring lessons to life, letting children explore the real world, build vocabulary and discover wonder far beyond the classroom walls.
