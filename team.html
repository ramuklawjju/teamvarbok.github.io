Our Teachers

Meet the People Who Make Varbok Special

Our educators are the warm hearts of Varbok. Trained, patient and endlessly loving, they help every child feel safe, seen and ready to bloom.

Ananya Krishnan

Founder & Lead Educator

A Montessori-trained educator with 18 years of experience, Ananya founded Varbok to give children the joyful, fearless childhood she always believed they deserved.

Rohan Mehta

Co-Founder & Curriculum Head

Rohan designs Varbok's play-based curriculum, blending global early-learning research with Indian values to make every lesson feel like an adventure rather than a chore.

Priya Nair

Kindergarten Coordinator

Warm, patient and endlessly creative, Priya leads our kindergarten programme and has a gift for helping shy children find their voice and their confidence.

Aditya Sharma

Music & Movement Teacher

A trained musician, Aditya turns mornings into magic with drums, songs and dance, helping little ones express themselves and fall in love with rhythm.

Sneha Reddy

Nursery Class Teacher

Sneha believes every child blooms in their own time. Her gentle, story-filled classroom is a favourite first home-away-from-home for Varbok's youngest learners.

Vikram Iyer

Arts & Crafts Specialist

Vikram runs our colourful art studio, where mess is celebrated and imagination rules. He helps children discover that there is no wrong way to create.

Meera Joshi

Child Counsellor & Caregiver

A trained early-childhood counsellor, Meera supports children's emotional growth with calm, kindness and care, ensuring every little one at Varbok feels safe and understood.

Kavya Pillai

Playgroup Lead Teacher

Bubbly and nurturing, Kavya welcomes our tiniest explorers with open arms, turning first goodbyes into giggles and helping toddlers settle into their very first classroom.
